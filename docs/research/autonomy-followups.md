# Autonomy follow-ups

A source audit identified paths where an owner's standing policy or shared
rule still decides behavior before an individual's alternatives are compared.
Revalidate these paths as the interval host changes. Fixes must retain the
physical effects and existing features, not replace cooperation with universal
consent or assign authority to institutions.

- Integrate social and other early scheduler phases with personal competition;
  preserve standing intentions and meaningful interruption costs. Continue to
  distinguish accidental encounters, deliberate approaches, and independent
  responses, including unwanted approaches and pressure.
- Fatigue and injury can limit physical capability, but shared fatigue
  thresholds should not automatically decide voluntary rest or waking. The
  elapsed-time migration must model physical transitions separately from a
  person's choice to rest.
- Replace general hunger and age gates on negotiation, delivery, and other
  discretionary actions with individual evaluation and relevant physical
  constraints. Existing personally chosen work already bypasses some legacy
  gates and must retain that behavior.
- Broaden ordinary social partner choice to reflect directed affinity,
  aversion, fear, goals, and circumstances without requiring consent for an
  encounter. Keep matching local and bounded rather than scanning all pairs.
- Skill lessons currently select a receptive learner implicitly. Model a
  person's attention, comprehension, and interests while allowing incidental
  learning. Exact recipe knowledge has separate individual learning choices;
  it does not replace the skill-learning path.
- Extend subjective interpretations to ordinary encounters, promise
  outcomes, and felt obligation. Existing signed personal reactions, including
  liking harm and feeling no obligation, must remain possible.
- Review family kin exclusions and the legacy automatic pairing fallback;
  distinguish personal or learned preferences from hereditary consequences.
  The current single-partner record limits relationship representation even
  though subjective conception does not require an exclusive partnership.
- Allow unrelated caregivers and individual decisions about claimed food,
  preserving physical access, custody, scarcity, care consequences, and
  pressure-motivated help.

Use feature-owned opportunities, subjective evaluation, typed intentions,
revalidation, and staged commits. Test differing choices in the same
circumstances, save/resume, and worker-count determinism. This list is not an
exhaustive proof of autonomy across the workspace.
