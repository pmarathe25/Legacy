# Follow-ups from long histories

Status: implementation completed; workspace verification is complete. Four continuing histories have a 400-year horizon
and a target of twenty further reproductive generations. Neither target has
been reached. These changes should improve the range of competent and
flawed characters, rather than guarantee survival or impose a social system.

The October 18, 2026 study uses ordinary biology, unscripted physical starts,
compact journals, and independently verified parentage. Its frozen runtime,
comparisons, checkpoints, and detailed observations live under
`target/history/centuries-2026-10-18/`. The performance report records the
remaining gap to the requested time and memory targets.

## Evidence

- The rainforest's first twenty years produced eight children from one parent
  pair. Seven died from deprivation; the eighth was still young at the checkpoint.
  Their mother had substantial altruism and empathy, some learned care values,
  and lower foresight and intelligence. This is one family's outcome, not proof
  that all parents refuse care.
- A diagnostic continuation followed the eighth child, Humanu, through death at
  roughly ten months. The journal recorded 117 feeds. In the final episode the
  infant was empty-handed in a different area from the caregiver, who had dried
  fruit. Local fruit also existed, but the child repeatedly failed actions and
  signalled need. Care intention, proximity, readiness, and delivery diverged.
- The warm world's first newborn died from damage six minutes after birth in a
  three-against-one fight. It was not hungry. Adult action and recruitment paths
  need developmental and consequence checks.
- Most surviving rainforest adults remained injured over many annual samples.
  Signal events were numerous, treatment was rare, and trade and distinct-work
  totals changed little after the initial burst. These correlations warrant
  tracing unresolved needs; they do not establish a single cause.
- Learning and voluntary transmission did occur: the first rainforest window
  retained 170 personal theory records, 106 sourced from other characters, and
  oral works reached audiences of up to fifteen. These are claims and exposures,
  not proof of truth or universal comprehension. Founders included high
  intelligence outliers; the problem is not simply the configured ceiling.

## 1. Close the dependent-care loop

Owners: `game-nurture`, `game-households`, `game-family-life`, `game-signals`,
`game-knowledge`, with physical delivery through existing receiver requests.

Trace how the child and caregivers separated, what each remembered, why food
acquisition failed, and which offers competed with returning or feeding. Keep
remembered dependent locations and expected needs across interrupted errands.
Combine incomplete signals with prior observations and expressed metabolism;
a different guessed need should not erase a supported hunger belief. Consider
travel, carrying, serving quantity, preservation, and the next expected visit as
parts of a care plan.

Test that capable, motivated caregivers can keep a child fed under ordinary
favorable conditions, including separation and save/load. Also test voluntary
refusal, mistaken beliefs, limited foresight, failed delivery, and neglect.
Do not give every parent the same priorities or inspect remote private needs.

## 2. Apply development and subjective risk consistently

Owners: `game-vitality`, physical action owners, `game-pressure`, and the
recipient's ordinary chooser for invitations and assistance.

Audit newborn proposals and the warm infant's battle path. Use expressed motor,
communication, cognitive, and learned capacities to determine available means.
Evaluate help and resistance against personal needs, injury, perceived danger,
relationships, and uncertainty. Incoming requests should support deliberate
recipient choices and ongoing joint commitments. Preserve reckless decisions,
coercion, refusal, and violence; avoid an imposed law or an arbitrary age ban.

Add composed regressions for infant movement, food acquisition, recruitment,
and combat, alongside unusually capable and impaired older characters.

## 3. Resolve needs through available means

Owners: `game-body`, `game-soma`, `game-metabolism`, `game-health`, `game-nutrition`,
`game-care`, `game-signals`, and `game-craft-planning`.

Distinguish acute wounds, temporary condition, aging, and lasting impairment.
Model biological recovery through constitution, recovery, rest, nutrition,
environment, and injury severity. Keep medicine useful and recovery uncertain.
A perceived medical need should be able to motivate obtaining or producing
supplies, rather than only requesting help when supplies already exist.

Let repeated signalling and unanswered requests affect learned expectations,
attention, fatigue, and recipient responses. Use consequences and preferences
instead of a universal leisure or signalling cooldown.

## 4. Connect personal experience to longer arcs

Owners: `game-observations`, `game-inquiry`, `game-testimony`, `game-works`,
`game-literacy`, and each consuming decision owner.

Extend observations and forecasts into care, danger, and social outcomes.
Only witnessed events and received reports should support conclusions. Make
personal discoveries useful to actual plans, teaching, and writing. Give works
and changing goals material from relationships, successes, losses, and revised
beliefs. Keep physical recipe possibilities separate from acquired know-how.
Do not inject prescribed plots or guarantee invention, monetary adoption, or
voting.

## Validation before a selection claim

Repeat several habitats after these capabilities are tested, retain failures,
and continue through twenty actual human reproductive generations where the
population persists. Twenty years, ecological generations, and immortal
survivors are not substitutes. Verify lineage, mortality, save/load replay,
resource costs, and bounded diagnostic output. Separate selection from drift,
family preferences, culture, and censored young cohorts.

## Implemented in the current iteration

- Deliberate manipulation and communication readiness constrain demands, assistance,
  combat orders, crafting, farming, feeding, treatment, and food opportunities.
  Tactical victims remain vulnerable; incapable fighters do not auto-attack.
- Assistance also weighs the helper's needs, perceived danger, and risk tolerance.
- Food observations survive competing cues, feeding intervals inform subjective
  appetite estimates, and a delivery can restart toward a personally remembered
  location. A hungry recipient can accept a meal while signalling need.
- Ordinary biology permits slow recovery during nourished rest, scaled by expressed
  recovery, with persistent fractional progress. It cannot restore lifespan or
  exceed current maximum health.
- Attention and compassion join the authored inherited traits. Attention affects
  switching costs and retained observations; development and acquired condition
  modify expression. Learned values and directed relationships remain distinct.

Focused regressions cover extreme-potential newborns, tactical vulnerability,
remote care and loading, competing need signals, and fractional healing. The
broader validation and history evidence belong under
`target/history/centuries-2026-10-18/`. The requested 200-year outcomes and further
performance targets are not yet established.

The remaining runtime gaps from this plan are implemented: displaced-dependent
searches, maintained delivery and accompanied-travel commitments, need-driven
medical supply production/acquisition, and personal care, conflict, and social
observations. Care practice and comprehensible manuals support cultural transmission;
works retain personal theories and memories. Questioning and registry consultation
connect unmet supply needs to voluntary testimony and actual written records.

Focused physics simulations separately exercise finite materials, apparatus,
load control, breakage, changing worker capability, interruption, and replay.
They establish physical invariants for these models, not demographic selection.
The long-history studies remain an ongoing empirical task with the explicitly
unreached horizons above; implementation completion does not claim those outcomes.

The [dependent-care follow-up](dependent-care-followup.md) records a verified
cooperation feedback defect and its matched post-birth correction. The
[autonomy follow-up](character-autonomy-followup.md) tracks delayed outcome
learning and encounter-based belief formation. Histories preserve their original
people, genes, ages, and journal prefix when an intentional model change starts
a new labeled phase; optimizer comparisons require identical outcomes.
