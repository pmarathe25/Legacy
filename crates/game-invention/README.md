# game-invention

Voluntary apparatus construction and experiments compete in the ordinary chooser.
Existing materials and visible geometry provide possible designs; private physical
limits do not become character knowledge. Trials expose moved loads, felt effort,
and travel to personal inquiry. Useful measured capacities can be retained and
transmitted through the shared knowledge system. Concern, curiosity, intelligence,
and ongoing commitments influence which opportunities are considered.

Remembered unsatisfied work requirements motivate experiments. Expressed intelligence
and attention bound design search, which can mix materials, vary transmission ratios,
and connect stages or branches. Familiar designs and measured devices remain available
for copying and retesting. Capacity records include the observed load and input effort;
material breaking limits stay private to physics.
