# game-society

`game-society` models social memory and institutions people can found, join, leave, and change through proposals and votes. A charter sets membership, decision thresholds, offices, jurisdiction, and rules. An office is effective only when its holder and enough supporters are living members. Members can also hear cases and choose enforcement policies and remedies.

The crate owns membership, charter validation, proposals, voting, and guarded resolution from scoped facts. Rules can grant institution-specific access or declare duties through the agreement owner. The world adapter applies foreign effects; people still choose compliance, and goods require physical delivery.

Personal charter judgments combine remembered rule outcomes with local stock knowledge, trust, and promised contributions. Founder responses are prepared in order before anyone acts; the crate reads borrowed facts without owning another feature’s traits.

Local discovery, hearings, duty work, case interpretations, and publication run from borrowed facts. Observers retain dated institution views; receipt interpretation attributes performed work once and uses only the person's known cases and stocks.

Institutions, proposals, concepts, cases, knowledge, search cooldowns, exclusions, and hearings are typed rows in the shared store. The runtime keeps only its receipt cursor. Prepared transitions guard their opening rows and stage one atomic batch; save validation receives referenced identities and agreement facts from their owners.

`personal` owns ordinary social admission, local teaching declarations, bounded attention, autonomous conversation choices, instruction completion, and reactions to births, care, play, production, and conversations. It receives factual contact, body, skill, stock, and agency inputs, prepares material and interaction effects, and calls Characters to receive relationships and taught routes. Tests exercise these interfaces directly with a shared store.

Characters owns canonical personal development, remembered teaching offers, chosen relationships, interaction reservations, and participation rows. Its social runtime retains rules and the review deadline. `review` selects institution office holders, founders, proposers, and locally available discovery cohorts from canonical rows; discovery and personal reviews build area indexes once and refresh only affected participants during a review.

Enforcement owns per-case `Case` and `Talk` rows, per-institution `Policies`, and per-person `Memory`. Its runtime retains the event cursor. Factual ports supply local people and foreign records; the owner prepares filings, responses, dispatch, judgments, support, perception, and reconciliation effects. An institutional adoption stages the projected proposal and its receiving policy or judgment in one transaction. Both owners validate the combined batch before canonical rows, reserved IDs, and events are published.

`rule_publication` prepares each institution's ordered access and duty effects together. The same guarded batch contains recognized duty links, real Agreement and Obligation rows, and dated institutional views; Skills and Realm can receive that publication before the host admits its IDs and events. `reactions::PreparedCaseReaction` similarly stages the case, witness learning, and exclusion bookkeeping under fresh-entity and source-row guards. Failed schemas and exhausted allocation budgets publish none of those effects.

`personal_receipts` prepares ordinary experience and reserved newborn development before an origin is published. `personal_completion` combines conversation or instruction changes with those experiences and an opaque Characters Mind builder. Cleanup and trait expression join the same batch through owner authorization; relationship, route, personal, and activity schema failures leave the origin and reservations untouched.
