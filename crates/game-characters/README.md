# game-characters

Characters have dispositions, relationships, aspirations, and remembered information. They rank available choices using their own knowledge and experience. Companionship, family plans, instruction, practice, and learned values can shape later decisions.

The crate owns personal judgments, plans, retries, learning, and social development. Other features supply factual opportunities, expected benefits, effort, uncertainty, and risk; Characters chooses among them. Executable actions remain opaque to its decision systems.

`Mind<A>` and inherited temperament are plain components in the shared store. Small typed interfaces expose observations and prepared changes. `MemoryLearner` receives historical stock and route information while retaining newer observations, current access rights, and existing routes. Temperament interprets opaque inherited alleles and combines predispositions with bounded experience. Biological aging and reproduction belong to Lifecycle; physical command admission belongs to the respective action owner.
