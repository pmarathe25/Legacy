# Tactical conflict in the economic world

Encounters use persistent tactical maps and actual local people. Incoming attacks are grouped by target in a cached parallel plan, and their simultaneous damage enters the same personal and family generation as hunger and disease. Combatants cannot perform other automatic actions during the round. The individual attacked does not need to agree to an encounter; their own reaction determines their initial response.

The host stages each round before publishing it, including withdrawals, deaths, attributed harm and changed tactical placements.

An autonomous fighter's optional `retreat_fear_at_least` lets their own directed fear of the opponent they chose to attack motivate retreat. The decision reads the committed prior-boundary relationship, so fear learned from an actual strike can affect a later round. A person without this preference is not forced to withdraw, and fear of an unrelated person is not used. Player-controlled fighters choose their own orders.

Individuals with an enabled confrontation preference may initiate a fight with someone local whom they personally distrust and do not fear beyond their tolerance. They consider at most 32 remembered relationships, and disjoint participants are selected deterministically in parallel. A targeted worker's active work is interrupted; both participants spend the boundary on the encounter. Existing combat rounds resolve first, so the new fight does not inflict damage until the next round. A controlled defender starts in defense and chooses subsequent orders. An explicit player action already submitted for that boundary takes priority over a new confrontation involving its participants.

An autonomous person's `interventions` preference names someone they would defend and an opponent. They may be willing, or act because their own fear of or obligation to a named person reaches a personal threshold. They need not have the beneficiary's consent. A local helper spends the boundary attempting to intervene and interrupts their work; after the current round they join the beneficiary's side and attack the named opponent only if both remain in the fight and an unoccupied entry position has line of sight to the beneficiary. Entry currently searches the first 64 coordinates on each map axis, the same bounded area used to start encounters. The helper does not strike until a later round. A failed late attempt still consumes the boundary and produces a failure receipt. Controlled people do not intervene autonomously. `economy-intervention` supplies an example.

A controlled person can instead deliberately join a local encounter on a named fighter's side against an opponent. Like initiating an attack, this changes the current tactical setup without advancing time; the next round reserves that person and interrupts any active work. Entry needs an open position with line of sight and never requires the beneficiary's consent.
