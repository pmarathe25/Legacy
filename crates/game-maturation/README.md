# game-maturation

Prepared physical batches cure or ferment while their maker can do other things. Workshop recipes produce inedible prepared goods; object tags specify the finished good, maturation period, and acceptable finishing temperature. The batch retains its original ready time when transferred. Ripening consumes the prepared quantity and produces the finished food or records a failed batch.

This is a small process model: finishing temperature is checked, rather than simulating microbial populations. Output mass cannot exceed prepared mass. Food preservation starts tracking the newly finished food. Saved batches reconcile against actual cargo.
