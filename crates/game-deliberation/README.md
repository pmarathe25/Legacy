# game-deliberation

An influence arrangement stores the people whose opinions count, an integer weight for each, and the support required for a decision. Characters can propose literal quantities through institutional requests. The bounded autonomous constructor uses the proposer's own values, dispositions and directed relationships; each addressed participant responds from their own interests and circumstances. These are executable arrangements about influence, rather than a menu of government types.

The constructor currently considers a bounded set of group members. It is an initial domain of institutional invention; it does not interpret arbitrary political prose. Different weights, participation and support requirements can express personal delegation, unequal influence, broad consultation or demanding collective agreement. Formation and later deliberation are separate from fulfillment and recognition of an institution's decisions.

`Personal`, `Leader`, `Majority` and `Unanimity` retain their historical save meanings. New autonomous joint arrangements use `Consultation` with explicit data. Losing contributors can leave an agreed threshold unattainable until surviving participants establish a change; no population or institutional survival is guaranteed.
