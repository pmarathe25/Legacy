# game-botany

Renewable plant patches contain small representative stands with inherited characteristics, varied ages, finite space, and real seed banks. Temperature, rain, development, age, and survival determine which plants produce and reproduce. Seeds inherit through cultivars; suitable surviving seeds recruit into vacancies. Failed populations can remain barren. No replacement plants are invented after the seed bank is lost.

Standing edible produce decays and retains genetic provenance. Gathering transfers the actual family lots into cargo. The stand is a bounded ecological approximation, rather than one simulated entity per blade of grass; plots likewise represent planted families. Plant histories use local strain identities and events so extinct plants need not remain in live state forever.
