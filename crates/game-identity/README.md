# game-identity

Retains explicit personal commitments, such as a chosen institutional arrangement.
The inherited `rigidity` trait, expressed through development, stress, and
upbringing, affects resistance to revising an established commitment. Commitment
content is acquired rather than inherited. Policy owners compare current and
proposed arrangements; no political system receives a preferred status.

Ordinary activities and care recipients do not become convictions merely because
they were chosen once. Their continuity depends on attention and actual work
commitments. Belief revision is handled by discourse and inquiry, including
extreme resistance to contradictory information.

State is bounded to 64 commitment domains per person and persists across save/load.
Typed `Commit` requests are the only mutation entry point.
