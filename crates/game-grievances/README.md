# game-grievances

Witnessed interference can change a particular observer's relationship with the actor. A witness considers claims they recognize, their empathy, and observable necessity. They can forgive or retain a grievance. Absent, sleeping, and dead people do not acquire the experience.

Retaliation is a later local decision that weighs grievance, aggression, patience, and fear. It requires a real capable actor, an available target, and ordinary conflict actions. No ambient criminal code or automatic sentence exists. Cultivation on another person's plot can also be noticed. Broader choices among negotiation, courts, avoidance, and enforcement are covered by the deferred [autonomy plan](../../plans/unimplemented/character-autonomy.md).
