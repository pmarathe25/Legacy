# game-wellbeing

Interprets physical injury, meals, completed rest, completed leisure, and conversations as personal experiences. Vitality owns condition, resilience, decay, and age-related expression. A conversation can relieve stress or cause distress according to each participant's own relationship. The participants need not agree. These small rules allow inherited predisposition and lived condition to remain distinct.

During childhood, a trusted conversation can gradually influence sharing, authority, and honesty attitudes. Acceptance depends on the listener's own relationship and empathy. Parenthood supplies no automatic transfer of beliefs. Vitality separately accumulates childhood deprivation and distress over elapsed time; neither these acquired conditions nor learned beliefs rewrite genes.
