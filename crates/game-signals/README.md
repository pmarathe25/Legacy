# Personal nonverbal signals

People independently decide whether to produce an available physical signal
during an actual encounter or direct it at a physically nearby person without
requiring that person's agreement to meet. Each person's signed inclinations and optional
hunger threshold govern their own expression; a signal can be produced even
when its private intended meaning does not match their bodily state. The
listener separately decides whether they notice it and interprets its form
using their own mapping, which can be absent or wrong. A retained observation
contains only what that listener noticed and inferred, not the sender's
private intention. No universal gesture-to-meaning mapping, mandatory response,
or institutional role is imposed.

The catalog names physical forms and possible personal meanings; it does not
assign meanings to forms. Encounters are disjoint and are prepared in parallel
from an immutable opening generation. The host checks actual proximity for
directed signals. Configured memories are bounded and optional; when many
signals arrive at once, the most recent retained observations use deterministic
actor order. Each observer can also assign signed relationship reactions to
their own inferred meanings. These can change affinity, trust, fear, or
obligation independently; no reaction is required, and the actor's private
intention is never used. An observer can also personally value giving a good
to the signaler after a particular interpretation; the interval host offers
that choice during the following physical interval. The giving owner checks
actual custody, capacity, reach and competing activities. No actual hunger,
request or agreement is inferred, and the giver may prefer idleness. This
currently uses the giving scheduler's fixed priority rather than the general
cross-domain choice round. An observer can also choose to associate a noticed
form with a personally configured meaning after receiving a real gift from the
signaler in the following interval. This is a fallible inference: the observer
can refuse or retain an existing mapping, and the sender's intention is never
consulted. Other learning evidence and physical responses remain unfinished.
An optional data-defined birth profile can
enroll actual newborns with individual, genetically adjusted expression
inclinations; it does not give them another person's interpretations,
relationships or memories. A newborn can be configured to direct a signal at
their actual mother, subject to physical reach and their own inclination.
The interval host enrolls them only when family birth enrollment is enabled.

Scenarios can author `[[signals.gifts]]` with a person, named catalog meaning,
named good, quantity, and personal value. These resolve to runtime identifiers
when the scenario is loaded; each preference belongs to that person's signal
mind. A configured giving owner is required to execute gifts.
`[[signals.learning]]` similarly names a listener, good and proposed meaning,
with an enabled preference, value and optional replacement permission. Only a
successfully transferred good supplies evidence; a proposed or failed gift does
not teach a mapping.
