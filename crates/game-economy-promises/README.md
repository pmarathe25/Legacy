# Personal promise commands

Validates individual proposals, responses, and changes of intention. A promise is a record of an interaction; it neither moves goods nor compels its maker to comply.

Proposals, responses, and explicit withdrawals are communicated locally. A person can privately change whether they intend to deliver at any time, without notifying the recipient or being compelled to comply.

An individual offer may set `after_help = true` to wait for that person's own
memory of a newly received food gift from the proposed recipient. The memory
is recorded only when the physical transfer succeeds, and the offer is made
on a later boundary. One receipt can motivate at most one such proposal. The
person may refuse to offer, the recipient may decline, and an accepted promise
can still be withdrawn, fulfilled, or defaulted according to later choices.
This is a personal inclination; it does not create a loan institution, debt,
or compulsory repayment.

A person's existing inclination to offer after witnessed requested work can
also respond to a personally witnessed cultivation fulfillment or refusal.
The remembered outcome selects a possible recipient; the person may still
decline to offer, and any resulting promise remains independently revocable.
