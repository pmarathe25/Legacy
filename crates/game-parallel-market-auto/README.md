# Parallel autonomous market

Opted-in recipients assess pending offers using their authored values. Only feasible offers enter deterministic priority matching; the lowest offer ID must be preferred by both participants for an exchange to proceed. Matching is conservative and may defer otherwise feasible offers. Missing valuations prevent acceptance, and equal value is accepted when minimum gain is zero.

A policy with `propose = true` also lets its person propose trades using their valuations and visible local carried goods. A proposal does not transfer goods: its recipient independently evaluates it at a later boundary. Missing valuations prevent proposals, and competing participants can defer each other under the conservative priority rule.

An offer can also be a one-sided request for a gift. A person with `request_help = true` may autonomously ask a local holder for a valued item, without offering anything in return. A recipient's `help_value` expresses their personal value for helping, while optional directed `influences` add subjective attachment, obligation and fear to their evaluation of the requester. These are personal estimates, not proof of approval, a threat, or an enforceable duty; a record that an exchange was accepted means that the physical action occurred, not that it was freely approved. An indifferent person can leave the same request unanswered. When `opinions.rules.gratitude_gain` is configured, directly receiving a gift can increase the recipient's felt obligation toward the giver. The opinions owner separately learns fear from actual harm when `opinions.rules.fear_per_damage` is configured; uninjured bystanders do not automatically learn it. These experiences can affect later choices, while attachment and anticipatory threats remain limited to authored estimates. See `content/parallel/cooperation.toml`.

For a full-tick scaling measurement with independent requests and learned relationships, run `cargo run --release -p game-parallel-fused-physical --example fused_combat_rounds -- 1 100000 spread cooperation 7`, and repeat with other worker counts. The timed section excludes world compilation and export.
