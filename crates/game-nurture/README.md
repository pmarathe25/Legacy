# game-nurture

Composes voluntary food care from personally observed need, attachment, learned concern, inherited dispositions, and the caregiver's own needs and commitments. It registers requests within the care owner and stores delivery intentions through household-owned requests.

Caregivers can obtain food without eating it, carry it along known roads, and serve a beneficiary from finite supplies. Partial harvests and servings remain useful. A distant beneficiary's last observed need supports an estimate; it does not reveal current hunger or location. Finding nobody at the remembered destination ends that delivery intention.

Action completion schedules care review after physical arrivals and before ordinary character review. A caregiver can abandon an errand as costs or personal needs change. Concern does not guarantee help or survival.

Physical travel, harvests, property use, food consumption, and bodily effects remain with their existing owners. This crate owns no independent plugin or persistent resource. Run the care, provisioning, ecology, and physiology feature tests.

With communication enabled, eligibility, prioritization, servings, stopping estimates, and reconsideration use the caregiver's perceived need. Successful offers reduce that estimate by expected relief; declined offers are observable feedback. The recipient and physiology owners retain true hunger and assent. Care therefore can be mistaken without acquiring an exact hunger oracle.
