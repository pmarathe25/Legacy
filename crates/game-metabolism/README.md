# game-metabolism

Derives energy demand, fatigue accumulation, and recovery from expressed characteristics. Genes affect potential through the trait and vitality owners. Development, aging, physical condition, stress, and thermal costs affect the resulting rates.

Infants have smaller absolute requirements and growing bodies approach adult demand. Large bodies and exceptional strength, constitution, or recovery have maintenance costs. High basal metabolism improves recovery while consuming more food; low basal metabolism saves food while slowing recovery.

The exposure owner implements the optional `Environment` query. This keeps climate and protection with their owners. The soma owner advances actual bodies, retaining fractional needs and recovery. No decisions or moral values belong here.

Run `cargo test -p game-metabolism` and the physiology development/forecast tests.
