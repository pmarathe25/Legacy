# game-food-storage

Characters build sealed food stores or cellars from crafted physical kits and spend time carrying surplus into them. Stores have finite cargo capacity and ordinary ownership claims. Construction consumes supplies and labor; interruption loses the paid kit. Moving food consumes handling time and retains its remaining freshness.

Ambient cold slows spoilage; heat accelerates it. Cellars use the region's time-weighted annual mean as a simple ground-temperature approximation. They do not create refrigeration. Sealed storage gives additional protection, and all shelf lives remain finite. Daily weather and real travel update the aging rate through preservation's typed conditions query.
