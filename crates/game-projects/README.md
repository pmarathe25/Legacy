# game-projects

`game-projects` lets people propose workshops and farms, obtain support, construct them, and arrange continued operation. Its state machines decide readiness, progress, cancellation, completion, and failure from supplied actor, land, stock, and agreement facts.

Completed projects become enterprises with accounts for inputs, outputs, labor, payments, and defaults. Prepared transitions check deadlines, counters, and stale records before committing. They request material consumption, timed work, and asset creation; the world applies those effects to physical inventories, sites, and fields.
