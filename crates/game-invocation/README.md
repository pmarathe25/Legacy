# game-invocation

Invocation draws on past lives' skills, goals, and memories. Activation pays fatigue and persistent burden; continued use adds exposure. Burden drives strain, emotion and identity effects. Prolonged severe exposure can permanently damage this incarnation's identity or lifespan. Historical mastery combines with current ability through owner-calculated work and combat rates.

The crate owns consent, warnings, control episodes, controller selection, costs, previews, and offered control actions. Burdens and allocated warnings are shared components; pending consent references its warning by ID. Guarded plans stage their component changes alongside Physiology's fatigue charge in one admitted batch. Direct penalties stay with the affected incarnation.

An accepted historical controller chooses physical intentions from its own personality and memories, with current memories taking priority. The application supplies body, scene, guidance, archive, and ongoing-action facts, then binds the returned intentions to physical owners. Historical content reaches Characters through its small `MemoryLearner` interface. Archives and record actions are still being moved from assembly into feature interfaces.
