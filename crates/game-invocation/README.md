# game-invocation

`game-invocation` defines the costs and benefits of drawing on past lives. Invocation adds burden and fatigue; continued use adds exposure. Burden bands set strain, emotional disruption, and identity effects. Sustained severe burden and exposure can cause permanent identity or lifespan loss. Rules calculate borrowed-work rates and recovery or cost thresholds.

The crate owns activation, warnings, consent, burden, control episodes, and competing controller selection. Accepted loss runs for its warned duration with costs continuing and dismissal unavailable. The world adapter applies body and lifespan effects, archives actual memories, and executes autonomous actions. Direct identity and lifespan penalties stay with the current incarnation.
