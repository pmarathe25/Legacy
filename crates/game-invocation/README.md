# game-invocation

`game-invocation` defines the costs and benefits of drawing on past lives. Invocation adds burden and fatigue; continued use adds exposure. Burden bands set strain, emotional disruption, and identity effects. Sustained severe burden and exposure can cause permanent identity or lifespan loss. Rules calculate borrowed-work rates and recovery or cost thresholds.

While the player retains control, the domain rules report costs requiring consent. Accepted loss of control runs for its warned duration, with costs and harmful outcomes continuing without renewed consent, dismissal, or protective caps. `game-sim` gates player actions and runs autonomous behavior during that interval.
