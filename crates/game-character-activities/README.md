# game-character-activities

Character-owned admission before broad frame deliberation, registered under the
existing characters owner. With `FrameTuning.activity_epochs`, a production
Gather first calls `Epoch(people)`. Idle actors with no personal food plan, full
health, fatigue above the rest threshold and hunger below the meal threshold may
start a physiology recovery activity together. Accepted actors leave the
collection cohort; remaining actors use ordinary decision preparation.

This intentionally changes priorities: eligible recovery precedes discretionary
providers, rather than competing with their utility scores. It may displace care,
work or discovery and must be evaluated as a new behavioral model. It does not
cache offers. Ongoing activity continuations live with their physical owner;
the first adapter is rest, not a universal replacement for all action families.

Admission checks player control and urgency before and after owner hooks. A
transaction-local urgency marker catches alerts that ordinary wake coalescing
would suppress. Failed batch admission rolls back before ordinary deliberation.
Alerts, damage, lineage transfer and disabling the setting cancel recovery through
its owner. Activity bookkeeping updates the normal mind schedule and engagement.
The configuration requires batched decisions, immediate urgent wakes and a
positive commit-group size. No new durable character state is introduced.

Profile-only `activity.rest.candidates` and `activity.rest.fallback` distinguish
provisional admission from owner execution. Measure these alongside full Collect
counts, total scheduled work, final artifacts, and end-to-end wall/CPU time;
parallel flag writes alone do not establish a throughput gain.
