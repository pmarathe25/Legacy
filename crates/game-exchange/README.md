# game-exchange

Offer enumeration reuses this person's unit values, marginal quantities, and sentimental costs while its stock snapshot is unchanged. Estimates are discarded before settlement; actual consent and fulfillment recheck current state. No cached price survives an encounter or exposes another person's private valuation.

Encountered people disclose bounded offers from their own packs and evaluate gains against their own possessions, needs, and personal acceptance memories. `Trade` permits different quantities on each side; the older `Barter` request delegates equal quantities. The search is bounded by expressed intelligence. Prices are local proposed ratios, without a fixed value table or designated money good.

Both parties must accept. Offers, acceptance, and fulfillment use agreements and property requests in one transaction; failed fulfillment rolls back both transfers. The demand owner learns only from completed agreements of which the observer was a party. Past acceptance can motivate acquiring an intermediate good for another exchange. Portability, spoilage, individual foresight, and fading evidence affect that estimate. Repeated acceptance may concentrate exchange on a medium; there is no universal currency or guarantee that it develops.

Wounded people can ask local caregivers for aid. Offers and requests do not grant access to another person's inventory. The encounter and family scheduling policies remain subject to the autonomy plan; negotiation currently evaluates exchanges immediately rather than spending time bargaining, and there is no mint, central bank, fiat decree, credit-money creation, or global market clearing.
