# game-cultivars

Plants use the same diploid alleles, inheritance, bounded mutation, and expression functions as characters. Sexual propagation recombines two parents; cuttings retain the parent's pair with mutation. Genes express yield, development speed, thermal and moisture tolerance, and lifespan. Larger harvest potential has a growth-time cost.

Physical seed and harvested-produce lots retain their lineage during transfers and seedkeeping. A plot represents a planted family; its genotype is sampled from the selected seed lot. Growers choose among seed lots using their own measured harvest productivity and a bounded search shaped by intelligence. They do not inspect the underlying genes. New strains retain parent identifiers; historical lineage can be retained in the research journal without storing every extinct plant in live state.
