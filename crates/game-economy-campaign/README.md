# Ruler lineage over the economic world

Stages the ruler's actual succession, invocation costs, and guardian journeys alongside personal life, travel, and physical custody. Warnings require an explicit game choice before a cost applies.

The interval campaign uses absolute times for deaths, subsequent births, and
invocation costs. A warning pauses advancement at the due instant until the
player accepts or dismisses it. An accepted cost can change health, shorten the
current lifespan, or suspend control. A successor has their own possessions
and relationships. Guardians use ordinary journeys and make their own
mentorship judgments. Older opening declarations create immortal people in the
same physical world and recover at the authored rate per simulated duration.
Ongoing confinement and journeys belong to those people's physical owners.

An unattended history follows the same lineage while releasing player inputs.
Each incarnation continues choosing food, work, and relationships when there
is no player. This setting survives checkpoints. Interactive campaigns still
hand control to the successor when they are born.

Start a headless session with `cargo run -p game-economy-cli -- --interval-campaign SCENARIO`.
Use `campaign` to inspect the lineage, `invoke PAST_LIVES` to invoke recorded
lives, and `accept-cost REVISION` or `dismiss` to respond to a warning.
`recall-life LIFE` shows the invoked predecessor's retained observations,
techniques, and language. During controlled work the incarnation can draw on
the strongest skill among the invoked lives. Invoking or dismissing lives
changes the worker's future rate at that instant, preserving completed work
and the contribution of any assistants. Learning remains personal.
