# Individual animal restraint

Owns physical holds and independent capture and release preferences. A hold links a living animal to a physically present person; it is not an ownership record or a promise of obedience. Animals can yield, resist, or be overpowered, and an actual escape attempt can weaken and break a hold. An individual can choose to release a local animal, including one held by someone else, or act from fear or felt obligation. Simultaneous releases contend per animal and take precedence over escape attempts; the current physical model does not represent the handler resisting release. The host must reconcile holds against committed person and animal locations and publish the resulting owner with the rest of the world.
