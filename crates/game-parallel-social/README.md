# Parallel social owner

This owner prepares local, mutually willing encounters from an immutable boundary snapshot. People who are traveling, dead, too ill, too hungry, or unwilling do not participate. Within each region, seeded identity priorities form disjoint pairs; each person participates at most once per eligible tick. Encounters increment a capped, undirected familiarity value and a checked counter. Sorted sparse relationship records and the latest tick's receipts are saved; older receipts must be streamed by a host if history is needed.

Participation is explicit per person, including newborns. This is a narrow social mechanism, not a model of friendship, subjective beliefs, speech, relationships with asymmetric opinions, family care, institutions, or player perception. It uses a pre-tick snapshot, so travel departures already in transit cannot meet; people who arrive in a tick first become eligible on the following tick. Prepared social changes publish only if the rest of the tick succeeds. Social-state validation, cloning, and accumulated relationship history still add cost each tick; no whole-game scaling claim follows from the parallel matching stage.

Optional `social.needs` rules model isolation per participant. Isolation rises while a person is alive and falls after a consenting local encounter; when contact is limited, the most isolated willing participants are considered first. Missing personal rows start at zero, and existing scenarios without this owner retain their prior matching behavior.

`social.rules.relationship_retention` optionally limits how many ticks a relationship may go without an encounter before it is forgotten. Forgetting is evaluated every tick, including ticks with no social matching. A later meeting creates a fresh relationship. The default is unlimited for previously authored content. With a finite window and at most one encounter per participant per social generation, retained new relationships are bounded by the number of participants times the window.

In the fused host, `talk PERSON TARGET` attempts a player-directed conversation and advances one tick. The target can decline, but their own optional fear or obligation preference can lead them to participate despite their normal willingness or personality. Physical availability is still required. A pressured encounter identifies the affected person; it does not automatically improve their trust or relieve their isolation. Preferences can be changed using `social-pressure PERSON enable|disable`. `social PERSON` shows the current ruler's own relationships in player mode. See `content/parallel/conversation.toml` and `content/parallel/social_pressure.toml`.
