# game-property

`game-property` stores claims about who may use, consume, transfer, or grant access to goods. Claims and recognition are social assertions; the crate tracks who supports or contests them and partitions real stock by provenance as goods move. It also records taking attempts and outcomes, plus each person’s dated, private knowledge from witnessing or hearing reports.

Actions cover requesting access, sharing, claiming, recognizing or contesting claims, taking goods over time, reporting incidents, and resisting a taking. Recognition does not itself grant title, permission, or punishment. The world layer applies actual stock transfers, access permissions, relationships, and combat effects.

Acquisition offers describe known, feasible alternatives. A character decides whether asking permission or attempting a taking fits their needs and values.
