# game-affiliation

Local encounters can invite newcomers into existing communities. Each individual chooses cooperation, loyalty, or compliance through fear; actual admission follows the institution's procedure. The institutions owner checks the required responses, while communities owns membership indexes and personal attachments.

People can leave arrangements they no longer accept. Departing custodians withdraw their old compact permissions; death clears membership and offices. A departed leader leaves personal decision rights until survivors accept another arrangement. Choosing ballots requires expressed interest in shared decisions. Leadership requires ambition and personally accepted authority. Procedure and membership changes publish events. Historical empty institutions remain inspectable.
