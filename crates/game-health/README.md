# game-health

`game-health` adds bodily needs, illness, care, and shelter rules to the game. It tracks thirst and cold exposure, accumulates enteric and respiratory infection, and models symptom timing, recovery, and treatment. Water lots retain contamination through collection, transfer, consumption, and purification; shelters track insulation, capacity, occupancy, and acknowledged access.

The crate owns action validation, work transitions, treatment consent, shelter admission, and clinical calculations. It plans water procurement from a person's remembered stocks, springs, and routes. The assembly adapter supplies physical and contact facts, translates typed effects, and orders shared transactions. Health owns stock access judgments, remembered-stock refresh, available action enumeration, clinical interval selection, finish readiness, and clinical reconciliation.

Local action admission distinguishes passive care from independent agency. It requests restriction, independence and resolved-position facts only when needed, so dependent or incapacitated people can participate in care without gaining physical actions.

All health state lives in the host's shared component store. Clinical state, jobs, occupancy, knowledge, and caregiver offers attach to person entities; water quality attaches to inventory entities and shelters use shelter entities. `Runtime` retains only rules and the last review time. Prepared actions guard their affected rows and exact related job, offer, and occupant membership, then apply one atomic batch before returning effects. Intrinsic schema checks validate each row's structure; catalog validation checks clock, person, stock, location, and activity references.

`prepare_interval` advances supplied clinical and job rows through the same numerical rules and retains sparse guarded changes. The host validates the prepared interval before publishing other frame writes, then commits through the atomic batch. Closing job policy can preserve intentional cancellation without accepting replacement work or recreating a removed job. `PreparedInterval::stage_into` exposes typed projected work deadlines before publication. The receiving adapter witnesses its concrete activity and body rows, stages deadline metadata beside the clinical and job writes, and validates both guards before one atomic apply. Receiver schema rejection therefore leaves all these rows unchanged.

Local exposure and observation systems consume temporary spatial, activity, body, and geometry facts. Health owns shelter inheritance, contact counting, symptom visibility, and remembered observations. Geometry providers supply visibility and reach facts without exposing their runtimes. Observations commit as a guarded batch and preserve water knowledge.

`prepare_local_index` builds one immutable index for a preparation. Physical systems can reuse its scalar exposure lookups, and perception can prepare observations from the same cohorts. Its borrowed store prevents using the index after health state changes.

Individual affordances can resolve shelter through a small carrier-chain lookup or inspect symptoms and pair visibility directly, without indexing the population.

Medical review owns work-pause decisions, care responses, accepted treatment instructions and healer selection. It preserves phase-opening work and offers while reading current physical availability, trust and patient contact. The adapter executes the selected intentions; review timing and publication bounds remain in Health.

Collected water mixes its contamination load with existing water and updates the holder's own observations. Clean water added to an unknown or unsafe stock does not erase that history. Prepared water and memory writes can join a cargo deposit in one guarded batch.

Prepared placements select the actual effective shelter through a small factual carrier/activity/body port. They guard every consulted actor, Occupancy and Shelter row, coalesce departures and compatible placements, and stage projected shelter observations in the same shared batch as motion. A receiving owner supplies projected physical facts; Health owns occupancy inheritance and visibility policy. `prepare_death_placements` or `PreparedPlacement::with_deaths` folds death removals into the same placement plan before staging, so stopped corpses and consulted dying carriers receive one Occupancy write while surviving passengers keep their physically valid shelter.

`prepare_water_reaction` receives opening material quantities and prepares exact contamination conservation together with subjective transfer or production knowledge. `prepare_water_received` owns intake dose and hydration; `prepare_care_water_received` combines that intake with its source's contamination debit. `with_water_consumption` supports composing the same debit into an existing owner plan. These plans stage alongside Cargo through one batch before either feature publishes. Zero-quantity production still invalidates water memory.

Prepared Health actions, reactions, cancellations, and placements retain sparse opening witnesses and a generic final batch guard. Call `validate_batch` after every participant stages. It checks exact owner writes, scoped membership, absent rows, and rules while allowing unrelated component edits. `operations` turns prepared effects into typed interruption, debit, and activity requests; the adapter translates activity representation and sequences the receiving owners.

Owner tests exercise the production action enumeration, reaction, interval, reconciliation, and staging entrypoints without assembly. A composed Cargo and Health intake test rejects a later invalid schema row without publishing either physical quantity or clinical exposure, then verifies successful hydration and conserved contamination.

Cancellation prepares one temporary borrowed Job and Offer index for all affected actors. Opening validation and final guards inspect the union of selected actor or shelter scopes once per component column in each phase; retained exact rows reject changes between scopes as well as new matching members. Unrelated scopes remain editable. The index is discarded with preparation and does not duplicate canonical state.

Run `cargo bench -p game-health --bench cancellation -- 3` to measure complete cancellation of 1,000, 2,000, and 4,000 coupled jobs and offers. CSV output separates preparation, opening validation, staging, final guard, and atomic application. Store construction and cloning are outside these timings; every sample verifies that each original work obligation is cleared once and that all selected jobs and offers are removed.

On the local optimized build, three samples produced median total cancellation times of 11.308 ms for 1,000 actors, 23.799 ms for 2,000, and 58.807 ms for 4,000. The corresponding preparation medians were 4.373, 6.024, and 12.818 ms; final guard medians were 1.020, 3.016, and 8.784 ms. Doubling population increased total time by 2.10 and 2.47 times. These serial owner measurements establish the tested workload's cost, not a linear scaling or frame-time guarantee; they exclude construction and cloning and do not measure the composed game's executor.

Clinical reconciliation also owns receiving water-safety reactions. An enteric illness onset marks a recent unpurified drink as suspected unsafe in the same prepared Clinical and Knowledge transaction. Both columns and the selected memory are guarded before publication; either schema rejecting the transition preserves both rows. The adapter marks already prepared illness events explicitly so event publication does not repeat this receiver.
