# game-exposure

Core temperature approaches a weather-dependent equilibrium over actual time. Worn clothing, wetness, shelter insulation, burning fuel, size, and expressed hardiness affect thermal stress. Discomfort costs fatigue and increases stress before severe cold or heat causes injury or death. Hardiness buffers thermal extremes in either direction, without acting as a source of heat. Conditions apply to player and autonomous bodies alike.

This is an integer game model with six-hour updates and finite thermal inertia, not a physiological temperature model. Clothing can be removed to avoid overheating. Indoor areas use ordinary movement and local observation; a fire cannot warm someone still outside.
