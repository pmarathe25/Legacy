# Personal dispositions

Authored sociability and diligence are stable personal traits on a 0–1000 scale. They influence voluntary social participation and automatic work without changing a player's direct commands. Isolation adds configurable urgency to social participation, so even an unsociable person can seek company when lonely. Deterministic counter-based sampling does not depend on worker scheduling. People without a record retain the ordinary unrestricted behavior.

Set `inherit_births = true` to give actual newborns the mean of their recorded parents' authored traits (or the only recorded parent's traits). If neither parent has a trait record, the child remains unrestricted. Inheritance occurs even when other automatic newborn enrollment is disabled.
