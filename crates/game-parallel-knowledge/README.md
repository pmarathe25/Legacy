# Parallel knowledge owner

Each configured mind keeps at most `max_records` observations of regional food. At a tick boundary, a living person observes the stock in their current region from the committed pre-tick state. A traveler receives no local observation. At an actual consenting local encounter, each party with a mind can pass at most one useful remote observation from their pre-tick memory; a report keeps the original witness and observation time and records its immediate sender. A report does not become current truth simply because it was heard. Direct observation takes precedence for the current region. When capacity is full, older unprotected records are evicted only for a newer report; the current local observation is protected.

Knowledge prepares against immutable state and publishes only after the physical and family phases succeed. A mind may privately configure `misreport = { food_delta = 50, target = 1 }` to change the quantity it tells one listener; omit `target` to address everyone. Negative offsets understate stock, and quantities saturate at zero and the maximum representable amount. This changes a statement, not the speaker's memory or the stock. Observation time and witness are claims carried from the remembered record, not independently verified provenance. The recipient sees the claim and immediate speaker, never an objective label identifying a lie. Honest reporting is the default. If `[opinions.rules]` sets `minimum_report_trust`, recipients ignore reports from a sender they distrust; direct observations remain available. The threshold is directed and uses committed trust, including trust changed by previous agreements. See `content/parallel/trust_reports.toml`.

The information model is deliberately limited to food stock. It does not model forgetting over time, perception errors, or private-property access. The fused host can enroll newborn minds and can use observations for travel decisions. Player observation exposes only the ruler's records.
