# Individual construction

Owns data-defined kit-to-site blueprints and personal construction choices. A person can assemble a local site only through an accepted physical kit consumption; fear or felt obligation can motivate an otherwise unwilling builder. Construction neither creates an organization nor compels anyone's participation.

An optional `salvage` quantity on a blueprint retains that many actual kit items in site custody instead of consuming them. Their expiry remains unchanged. After successful destruction, the surviving contents become local debris and can be recovered by the ruler or by independently willing or pressured people. Recovery uses ordinary capacity-limited physical transfers; exhausted debris custody is retired.
