# game-grammar

Pure inference and interpretation of personal relational constructions. The caller
supplies words, independently perceived scenes, cognitive capacity, and feedback.
There is no universal word order or inherited grammatical content.

Repeated evidence with distinct contexts makes constructions productive. Ambiguity
can motivate role particles. Established constructions and sufficient cognitive
capacity support embedded clauses. Learners interpret foreign sentences through
their own rules and vocabulary; conflicting parses remain unknown. Each grammar
keeps at most 64 constructions and eight compact contrast fingerprints per rule.
