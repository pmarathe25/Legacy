# game-demand

Characters remember goods that counterparties accepted in completed agreements they personally joined. Private evidence decays with absence and is bounded to 64 counterparties per good. Replaying an old settlement cannot add evidence. There is no currency field, global price table, money balance, or privileged commodity.

Expected resale value combines this evidence with personal foresight, physical mass, and food durability. A character can therefore acquire something for another exchange. Local exchange can concentrate on a medium when acceptance spreads, or remain diverse barter. This does not guarantee money emerges: no acceptance evidence or repeated exchange means no resale premium. Estimates currently generalize across future counterparties; trust networks and testimony remain further work.
