# game-shelter

Shelters are ordinary indoor areas linked to the outdoors by a real, timed doorway. Existing movement, local observation, stock access, and conversation therefore respect room boundaries without another spatial model. Builders consume crafted shelter kits and labor, and choose names from their own identities. The room contains a finite claimed store; claims do not forbid entering the area.

Fires consume actual fuel and heat the room for a finite duration. Exhaustion restores its outdoor-derived climate. Insulation protects occupants from heat loss; it does not create warm air. Indoor rainfall is zero. Regional climate changes propagate into rooms, while growing crops retain exposure accrued before a heating change.
