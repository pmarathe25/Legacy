# Local stores and carrying

Distinct named stores have their own capacity and dated physical custody. Individuals can choose to pick up goods and deposit them at another store or regional stock on separate boundaries. The actor retains custody between actions and may stop, while physical transactions resolve competing uses and preserve expiry. Fear or felt obligation may independently motivate a carrier; no organization or agreement compels them.

Optional individual goal-fetching preferences allow people to seek missing inputs for their own active work goals in observed stores in their current region. They carry and deposit the actual goods across separate boundaries, can stop at any time, and do not gain knowledge of remote stores. Existing direct work choices take precedence.

When personal input planning is enabled, the search also follows bounded recipe dependencies to locate supplies for intermediate work. Cycles and the number of alternatives explored per person are bounded, and available alternatives rotate between reviews.

An optional `walking` model adds directed, data-defined travel times between regional stock and named stores. A person has an individual position or a paused walking leg; goods stay in their actual carried custody while they walk. A carrying preference can independently motivate walking to its source and destination, and the transfer requires a later boundary after arrival. Changing willingness can pause the leg. A separate authored personal visit preference can also motivate a person to walk without a carrying task, including through their own fear or felt obligation. It does not reveal the destination contents, and a missing route leaves the person free to choose another activity. This first logistics slice conservatively pauses many autonomous discretionary activities while the person is away from stock; local carrying, food, goal-input and expedition-supply pickup from the current store, reading or writing carried pages, repairing carried tools, using carried medicine for wounds or disease, giving carried goods, asking for food, and encounters remain available; it does not yet model street-level social proximity or automatically route goal-fetching, food seeking, or expeditions to stores. A goal fetcher who has obtained an input can independently choose to carry it back to regional stock, then deposit it and pursue their work goal. Those planners cannot inspect or take from remote named stores while this model is enabled. At a store, a goal fetcher can choose direct inputs for their own goal from visible goods without consulting distant regional stock; recursive input planning still happens at regional stock. An enabled personal work goal takes priority over a standing visit when its inputs are ready or can be deposited; explicit carrying choices remain separate. Regional departure requires reaching the stock node.

The ignored `local_walking_and_contended_pickup_benchmark` exercises a full boundary for 100,000 people and a subsequent shared-store pickup. On a Ryzen 5 7600, one and six workers measured 46.0 and 17.6 ms for walking, and 136.1 and 53.5 ms for pickup; both runs produced the same state fingerprint. These are workload-specific measurements, not a linear-scaling guarantee.

The existing concentrated goal-fetch and expedition-acquisition benchmarks accept `LEGACY_AT_STORE=1` to place actors at a contested named store. On a Ryzen 5 7600 with 100,000 people, one and six workers measured 150.8 and 58.4 ms for goal fetching, and 122.3 and 45.1 ms for expedition acquisition; each pair produced identical state fingerprints.

The existing tool-repair and autonomous-wound-care benchmarks also accept `LEGACY_AT_STORE=1`; set `LEGACY_TOOL_REPAIRS=1` for the tool workload. On a Ryzen 5 7600 with 100,000 people, one and six workers measured 208.5 and 69.6 ms for repairs, and 204.6 and 90.7 ms for wound care. Each pair produced identical state fingerprints.

The existing concentrated giving and disease benchmarks accept `LEGACY_AT_STORE=1`; add `LEGACY_SELF_TREATMENT=1` to exercise disease self-treatment. On a Ryzen 5 7600 with 100,000 people, one and six workers measured 111.2 and 54.0 ms for one giving boundary and 283.4 and 112.1 ms for three disease boundaries. Each pair produced identical state fingerprints.

The concentrated food-request benchmark accepts `LEGACY_AT_STORE=1`. On a Ryzen 5 7600 with 100,000 people, the one- and six-worker medians were 50.2 and 18.3 ms, with identical state fingerprints.

With `LEGACY_AT_STORE=1 LEGACY_GOAL_RETURN=1`, the concentrated goal-fetch benchmark first acquires inputs and then times the return-walking boundary. For 100,000 people on a Ryzen 5 7600, one and six workers measured 90.4 and 31.0 ms with the same state fingerprint. The preceding contested pickup measured 161.1 and 60.7 ms.
