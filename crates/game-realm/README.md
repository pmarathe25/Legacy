# game-realm

`game-realm` records regional commitments between institutions. Proposals can promise deliveries under existing agreements, grant passage through named areas, or provide patrols through missions. Each participant's institution ratifies through its own proposal and decision process before any term can proceed.

Ratified terms remain pending until the relevant owner supplies a real agreement, territorial permission, or mission receipt; patrols describe work and never guarantee safety. People learn commitments through dated private reports, so each person's view reflects what they have received rather than a globally refreshed record. The crate tracks commitments and evidence while the owning systems perform the work.
