# Personal expeditions over dated custody

A traveler with a personal knowledge mind can set an objective to observe food at the destination. It is completed only after their own fresh local observation, not by an earlier report, another person's observation, or merely departing. The remembered observation can later be communicated under the traveler's own reporting choice.

Stages individual expeditions against real routes, local availability and the economic world's travel state. Departures preserve actual personal goods and never move someone whose physical circumstances no longer permit the trip. A meeting goal requires a real local encounter. An invited companion may participate voluntarily or respond to directed fear or obligation; they reassess that choice during the journey and can stop participating. Cancellation leaves any ongoing physical journey intact. Expedition routes currently require durations representable as `u32` ticks.

A traveler may deliberately seek the person named by their meeting objective at the destination, either willingly or because of their own fear or felt obligation. The other person independently responds according to their own willingness or directed fear or felt obligation. Both can act under pressure. Competing meeting attempts reserve each participant at most once; a refusal does not complete the objective.

A delivery objective tracks actual accepted gifts and independently fulfilled promise transfers from the traveler to a specified person at the destination. Partial deliveries accumulate across boundaries and saves; wrong recipients, failed transfers, taking, unfulfilled promises, and transfers elsewhere do not count. The traveler still decides whether to deliver, and can be motivated by their own fear or felt obligation. Completing an objective does not guarantee that the recipient retains the goods.

An optional per-expedition `delivery_preference` enables an autonomous traveler to give toward their own objective after reaching the destination. `willing`, `reserve`, and an optional `pressure_threshold` are individual settings that can be changed during the journey. The traveler retains the configured reserve, and fear or felt obligation toward the recipient can motivate an unwilling person. A missing preference creates no automatic delivery.

The preference may contain an independent `acquisition` choice. A willing or personally pressured traveler can take missing goods from observed regional stock or a named local store into their own custody before continuing the journey, subject to capacity and physical arbitration. `disregard_claims` lets them choose to overlook competing regional stock claims; otherwise their ordinary claim preference applies. Named stores have separate custody. A failed or disabled acquisition does not create goods or bind the traveler to try again.

When walking is enabled, an acquisition may specify `known_store` as a personally known local destination. Before reaching the expedition destination, the traveler can walk there when missing supplies are unavailable at their current location, without seeing the store's contents beforehand. Pickup and a possible return to the regional exit take additional boundaries; an empty store does not force a return. The preference can be changed during travel.

Set `LEGACY_MISSION_SEARCH=1` on the existing concentrated expedition-acquisition benchmark to measure departures toward a known store. On a Ryzen 5 7600, 100,000 travelers took 70.5 ms with one worker and 24.2 ms with six; state fingerprints matched. These results are specific to the workload and sublinear.
